Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Khayal Studio (“Khayal”, “we”) is an AI film and image studio based in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or work with us, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, email or Instagram, including your name, brand, phone number and project details.
  • Files you share for a project, such as product photos, logos, brand guidelines and briefs.
  • Basic website statistics, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not sell personal data.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, prepare concepts and quotes, and deliver your project.
  • To create your content. Your files may be processed by the AI generation and editing tools we use to produce your work, only for your project.
  • To send invoices and keep the business records we are required to keep.

What we do not do

  • We do not publish, share or show your files or unreleased work to anyone without your written permission.
  • We do not use your files to build our own datasets or to train our own models.

How long we keep it

We keep project files for as long as needed to deliver and support your project, and invoices for as long as UAE law requires. You can ask us to delete your files at any time after delivery.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data. Email anas@nxor.ai and we will reply within 30 days.
